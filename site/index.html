Where this data comes from
  • Elvebredd: the full value list, and every change on elvebredd.com/value-updates since September 2023.
  • AMVGG: the full value list (pets and every other category), and its value-updates feed since 2 June 2026.
  • Completed trades: amvgg.com/trades/completed, checked every 15 minutes.
