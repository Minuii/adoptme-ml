The newest offers (trade listings) from the AMVGG and Elvebredd trade centres. They're collected every 15 minutes, and this page refreshes every hour. Trader names are left out. "Offering" is what the poster gives; "Wants" is what they ask for. Values come from the list you pick. If that list doesn't carry an item, or the offer asks for something like "Add", the offer can't be valued.

Where this data comes from
  • Elvebredd: the full value list, and every change on elvebredd.com/value-updates since September 2023.
  • AMVGG: the full value list (pets and every other category), and its value-updates feed since 2 June 2026.
  • Completed trades: amvgg.com/trades/completed, checked every 15 minutes.
